Privacy policy

QVRLite has no internet access, no location access and no servers.

In short

The developer collects nothing. QVRLite has no internet or location permission, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. This website has none either.

What stays on your phone

Your recordings, recording icons and settings are kept on the phone. Private recordings are deleted if you uninstall QVRLite or clear its data, so export anything you want to keep.

Children

QVRLite is not aimed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on GitHub. This policy was last updated on 21 November 2026.
